Privacy policy

How {{ company }} handles information in BizPilot.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, business name, business type, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Your records: the sales, expenses, services, prices and notes you enter.
  • Technical information: security events such as logins, with the IP address and time, kept for 90 days to protect accounts.

How it is protected

Your records are encrypted on our server (AES-256-GCM) with a key unique to your account, and that key is itself protected by a master key kept apart from the database. Data travels between your device and our server over an encrypted connection. Your email address, business name, business type and record dates are stored unencrypted so we can run the service. Records are also kept on your own device, so protect your device with a screen lock.

Why we use it

To run your account, back up and sync your records, manage your trial and subscription, keep the service secure, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your information and we do not show adverts.

Who can see it

People who can log in to your account. We only look at your records if you ask us for support or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your account exists, and at least 90 days after a subscription ends. You can delete your account and all its records yourself from Settings, or ask us to.
